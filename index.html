WELCOME TO THE CLUB 01 / ABOUT US

MORE THAN
JUST FOOTBALL.

At Millbrook Swifts, we believe football is about more than just the scoreline — it’s about community, sportsmanship, and a relentless passion for the beautiful game.

From our grassroots youth squads taking their first touches on the pitch to our senior teams competing week in and week out, we are proud to represent Millbrook with heart, grit, and determination.
